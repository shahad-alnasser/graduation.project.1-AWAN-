# Awan | أوان
### Smart Cooperative Training Platform

> **Project in Progress**
> Awan is a university graduation project currently under development. The features described below represent the proposed system and may change as development continues.

## Overview

Awan is a proposed web platform that connects students, companies, company supervisors, university supervisors, and the university training unit in one centralized system.

The project aims to simplify the cooperative training journey, from finding and applying for opportunities to communication, report submission, progress monitoring, and student evaluation. It also proposes AI-based recommendations to help students find opportunities that match their profiles.

## Proposed Features

- Search for training opportunities and submit applications.
- Receive AI-based opportunity recommendations.
- Post opportunities and manage applicants.
- Review and approve training opportunities.
- Assign supervisors to students.
- Exchange messages and receive notifications.
- Submit and review progress reports.
- Monitor training progress and evaluate students.

## User Roles

| Role | Main Responsibilities |
| --- | --- |
| Student | Find opportunities, apply, submit reports, and follow training progress. |
| Company | Post opportunities and manage applications. |
| Company Supervisor | Supervise assigned students and evaluate their performance. |
| University Supervisor | Follow up with students and review their progress. |
| University Training Unit | Approve opportunities and coordinate supervision and training activities. |
